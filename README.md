# 🏟️ Female Football Overuse Injury Prevention

> **Evidence-based prevention protocols for stress fractures and tendinopathy in female football/soccer athletes** — synthesized from clinical trials registered on [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov).

---

## 📋 About This Repository

This repository consolidates findings from prospective cohort studies, randomized controlled trials (RCTs), and observational research registered on ClinicalTrials.gov, focusing on **overuse injuries** — specifically **stress fractures** and **tendinopathy** — in **female football (soccer) athletes** across all competitive levels (youth, academy, professional).

The goal is to provide coaches, sports medicine professionals, physiotherapists, and athletes with a single, organized, evidence-backed reference for injury prevention planning.
